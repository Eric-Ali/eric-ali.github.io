Celebration FAQ

What should I wear?
Dressy casual encouraged, and something comfortable for lots of eating and dancing! If it helps, Ali will be wearing a white dress and Eric will be in a suit.

Can I bring my kids?
We’d love to have your little ones join! We’ll have a coloring and game corner, and some kid friendly pizza options.

What’s for dinner?
Sonny’s is our favorite neighborhood place for delicious pizza, which will be served buffet style alongside salads and other snacky appetizers and an open bar, come hungry! If you have any dietary restrictions, please note that in your RSVP!

How should I get to Sonny’s?
There is extremely limited street parking nearby, we recommend walking from the Columbia Heights Metro (~10 min).

Do you have a wedding registry?
We kindly request NO gifts. If you must, we invite you to donate to some of our favorite organizations doing good work – World Central Kitchen, NPR, and the Washington Area Bicyclist Association.

When should I RSVP by?

Travel Details

Airports
The D.C. area has 3 airports – National Airport (DCA), Dulles International (IAD) and Baltimore Washington International (BWI). All are public transit accessible, however DCA will get you closest to the city.

Where to Stay

  • The Line
  • AirBnB: Book early, but lots of options in the Mt. Pleasant, Columbia Heights, and Adams Morgan area. If you’re unsure about a location, send Eric or Ali a text and we can help!
  • Holiday Inn Washington-Central
  • Double Tree Silver Spring: right over the border in Maryland, but an easy bus takes you to the venue.

    • Getting Around
    We recommend utilizing the transit system to get around, the metro is easy to use and can take you most places – more info on the WMATA website. (Metro cards are no longer needed, just tap a credit card or your phone with a virtual card to ride the bus or train!)

    Depending on where you are staying, finding parking can be difficult. We recommend skipping the rental car and utilizing lyft, uber, or taxis (through the curb app) when needed. However transit, or biking (rental bikes through Capital Bikeshare) or walking are often the fastest way to get places.
